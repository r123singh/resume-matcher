Position Overview:

We are seeking a highly skilled Product Manager with mandatory experience in the EdTech industry. The ideal candidate will have a strong understanding of the product development lifecycle, particularly within the educational technology domain. You will lead the creation, development, and enhancement of educational products, ensuring alignment with both business and educational objectives. This role involves close collaboration with cross-functional teams (engineering, design, marketing, and sales) to deliver products that not only meet market demands but also improve learning experiences and drive customer satisfaction.

Key Responsibilities:

Product Strategy and Vision:

Define and communicate a clear product vision that aligns with Academian’s broader goals, focusing on addressing key educational challenges.
Identify and prioritize product opportunities based on thorough market research, customer feedback, and analysis of trends in the EdTech space.
Create and manage a product roadmap that clearly articulates strategic goals and timelines for key stakeholders.
Product Development Lifecycle Management:

Oversee the entire product development process—from ideation to launch—specifically within the EdTech space.
Collaborate closely with engineering, design, and QA teams to define product requirements, prioritize backlogs, and ensure timely delivery of features that enhance learning outcomes.
Lead sprint planning, release management, and post-launch activities to meet the needs of educators, students, and institutions.
Customer Focus and Market Research:

Conduct in-depth market research and gather valuable insights from educators, students, and institutions to inform product strategy and identify new opportunities.
Engage with key educational stakeholders to understand their pain points and ensure that the product effectively addresses these challenges.
Monitor emerging trends and competitor products in the EdTech sector to keep Academian's offerings innovative and competitive.
Stakeholder Communication:

Serve as the primary point of contact for all product-related inquiries and updates.
Present product progress, key metrics, and roadmap updates to internal stakeholders, including executives and cross-functional teams.
Collaborate with marketing and sales teams to develop go-to-market strategies and ensure successful product launches, with a focus on the educational market.
Performance Monitoring and Optimization:

Define and track key performance indicators (KPIs) to assess product success and inform future enhancements.
Analyze post-launch performance and incorporate feedback from educators and students to continuously improve product features and functionalities.
Use data-driven insights to guide ongoing product improvements and optimize user experiences.
Qualifications:

Bachelor’s degree in Business, Engineering, Computer Science, Education Technology, or a related field; an MBA or advanced degree is a plus.
3-5+ years of product management experience, with a proven track record in the EdTech industry and managing products through the entire development lifecycle.
Deep understanding of agile methodologies and experience working with agile product development teams.
Exceptional communication, presentation, and interpersonal skills, with the ability to inspire and influence cross-functional teams.
Strong analytical skills and the ability to leverage data for decision-making.
Familiarity with product management tools like JIRA, Trello, or similar platforms.
Ability to thrive in a fast-paced environment and manage multiple priorities effectively.
Passionate about building products that positively impact education and drive meaningful learning outcomes.
